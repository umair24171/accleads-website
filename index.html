CSRS 4200 Compilation Engagement Support

Compilation files prepared to CSRS 4200, ready for your review

Since periods ending on or after December 14, 2021, compilation engagements in Canada follow CSRS 4200, which replaced the old Notice to Reader. We prepare the compiled financial information and the supporting file. Your firm accepts the engagement, reviews our work and issues the Compilation Engagement Report.

Get a fixed quote

What's included

  • Draft balance sheet, income statement and retained earnings statement
  • Note describing the basis of accounting used
  • Lead sheets and supporting schedules for each balance
  • Adjusting entries proposed and documented for your approval
  • Points for your attention flagged in a review memo
  • Files prepared in your firm's working-paper software where possible

AccLEADS prepares the compiled information and working papers. The compilation engagement itself, including acceptance, practitioner review and the Compilation Engagement Report, is performed and issued by a licensed public accounting firm.

Who it's for

What we'll need

  • Prior-year compiled statements

FAQ

Questions owners and firms ask us

Do you work with businesses anywhere in Canada?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across Canada. Tell us your province during the free review so we can confirm we cover any provincial filings you need.

I'm two years behind on my books and taxes. Where do we start?

With a free review. We look at your bank statements, prior returns and any CRA letters, then quote a fixed fee to rebuild the missing years and file what's overdue. Getting current is usually less painful than owners expect.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work in QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage and Wave. If you don't have software yet, we'll recommend one and set it up.

What is the difference between a compilation engagement and a Notice to Reader?

They are the same type of service. Since periods ending on or after December 14, 2021, Canadian standard CSRS 4200 replaced the Notice to Reader with a Compilation Engagement Report and added a required note describing the basis of accounting. A compilation provides no assurance; it is not an audit or a review.

Can AccLEADS issue a compilation engagement report?

[Edit to match your licensing.] We prepare the compiled financial statements and working papers. The engagement report is issued by a licensed public accounting firm, either your existing CPA or one we coordinate with.

When are my T4 and T5 slips due?

By the last day of February for the previous calendar year, both to the CRA and to the people receiving them. If that date falls on a weekend, the next business day applies. Filing more than five slips of a type requires electronic filing.

When is my corporation's T2 due?

Six months after your fiscal year-end. Tax owing is due sooner: two months after year-end, or three months for many Canadian-controlled private corporations that qualify for the small business deduction.

How do you charge?

Monthly services have a fixed monthly fee. Catch-up and year-end projects have a fixed project fee. You receive the quote in writing before any work begins.

How do you keep my information secure?

Documents are exchanged through a secure client portal, access is limited to the staff working on your file, and team members sign confidentiality agreements. We never ask you to send banking passwords.

I'm a CPA firm. Will my client know you prepared the file?

Not unless you tell them. We work under your firm's name and process, and your client relationship stays entirely yours.
